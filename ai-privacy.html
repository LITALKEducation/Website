AI Chat Privacy Notice

What we collect when you chat with น้องลิลลี่, why, who else sees it, and how to ask us to delete it. This adds to our general Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

1. What this notice covers

This AI Chat Privacy Notice explains what we collect when you chat with น้องลิลลี่ ("Nong Lilly"), why, and what happens to it. It supplements our general Privacy Policy, which continues to apply — this notice only adds detail specific to the AI assistant. LITALK Education is the data controller, and we handle personal data in accordance with Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

2. What we collect

For every conversation, on every surface, we store:

  • The messages you send and the replies the assistant generates, in full.
  • A conversation identifier that groups the messages of one chat together, and the date and time of each message.
  • Which surface the conversation came from (website, Ask a Word, Student Portal, or staff panel).

In addition, depending on where you are chatting:

  • On the public website and the Ask a Word page, we store a visitor ID — a random identifier your browser generates and keeps in local storage. It exists only so we can apply the daily message limit and detect abuse. Unless you sign in, it is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • In the Student Portal, we store the student ID the conversation is about, and we send the assistant a limited snapshot of that student’s account — name or nickname, course, credit balance, upcoming classes, and recent or pending payments — so it can answer. We never send another student’s data.
  • In the staff panel, we store the email address of the staff member using it.

Your acceptance of the AI Chat Terms

When you accept the AI Chat Terms on the public website or the Ask a Word page, we record your visitor ID, the version of the terms you accepted, the language they were shown in, and the date and time. We keep this so we have a record of the acceptance — a record held only in your own browser would not be one.

If you sign in on Ask a Word

LITALK students may sign in on the Ask a Word page with their @litalkeducation.com account. Signing in is optional — the page works without it — but when you do, we store your student ID alongside the questions you ask, so that you can read them back later from that page or from the Student Portal. Your daily message limit then follows your account rather than your browser.

Signed in, those questions are personal data connected to you, and everything in this notice about access, correction and deletion applies to them. You can delete any saved conversation yourself from the Student Portal without having to write to us.

What we do not collect here

The public website and Ask a Word assistants do not ask for and do not require your name, email address, or phone number. Please do not type sensitive information such as passwords, ID or passport numbers, card or bank account numbers, or health information into the chat. If you do, it will be stored along with the rest of the message — contact us and we will delete it.

3. Why we collect it

  • To generate a reply, and to give the assistant enough of the earlier conversation to follow what you are asking about.
  • To apply daily message limits and protect the service from abuse and excessive cost.
  • To keep a record that you accepted the AI Chat Terms.
  • So our staff can review conversations to see what people ask, correct wrong or unhelpful answers, and improve the assistant and our teaching materials.
  • To follow up on a genuine enquiry, and to comply with our legal obligations.

Our legal bases under the PDPA are your consent (which you give by accepting the AI Chat Terms), the performance of our contract with you where you are an enrolled student, and our legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving the service.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your conversations to train third-party AI models.

4. Who else sees it

  • Google, through the Gemini API (Google Cloud), which generates the replies. Your message and the recent conversation are sent to Google for processing each time you send something. For Student Portal chats this includes the limited account snapshot described above.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the service and the database the conversations are stored in.
  • Authorised LITALK Education staff, who can read conversation logs in our admin panel for the purposes described above.
  • Authorities or advisers, where we are legally required to disclose.

These providers process data outside Thailand. Where personal data is transferred internationally we take steps to ensure an adequate level of protection, as the PDPA requires.

5. How long we keep it

We keep chat messages for six months from the date they are sent. After that they are deleted automatically by a job that runs every day — we do not keep them indefinitely and we do not archive them elsewhere.

Your record of accepting the AI Chat Terms is kept for as long as that acceptance is the one we are relying on. When we publish a new version of the terms and you accept it, the previous record becomes history and is deleted six months later.

You can remove a conversation sooner. If you were signed in, you can delete it yourself from the Student Portal; otherwise ask us, and we will do so as long as you can give us enough detail to identify it — see "Your rights" below.

Clearing your browser’s storage removes the visitor ID and your acceptance from your device, so the chat will ask you to accept the terms again. It does not delete the conversations already stored on our side — for that, please contact us.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask us to give you a copy of your chat data, correct it, delete it, or restrict or object to how we use it, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out, and means you will not be able to keep using the assistant.

For chats on the public website or the Ask a Word page we can only find your conversations if you can give us the visitor ID stored in your browser, or enough detail to identify them — because we deliberately do not link those chats to your identity. Please contact us before clearing your browser storage if you intend to make a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee.

7. Children’s data

Many of our learners are children. Where a user is a minor, we rely on the consent of a parent or legal guardian, given when they accept the AI Chat Terms or register the student. A parent or guardian may exercise any of the rights above on the minor’s behalf.

8. Changes to this notice

We will post any update on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your chat data, we will ask you to accept the AI Chat Terms again before you continue using the assistant.

9. Contact us

To exercise any of your rights, or to ask a question about this notice, contact us through the LITALK Education LINE Official Account or the contact details on our website.
