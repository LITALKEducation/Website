AI Chat Privacy Notice

What LITALK collects when you use Nong Lilly and other LITALK AI chat surfaces, why we use it, who processes it and how long we keep it.

Last Updated: August 25, 2026 · Effective Date: August 25, 2026

1. Scope

This notice supplements our Privacy Policy and applies to Nong Lilly and LITALK-operated AI chat features on the public website, Ask a Word, Student Portal and authorised staff surfaces. LITALK Education is the data controller for the processing described here where we determine its purposes and means.

2. Data we process

  • Chat content: the messages you send, AI replies, conversation identifier and message timestamps.
  • Surface and service metadata: whether the conversation came from the public website, Ask a Word, Student Portal or an authorised staff surface.
  • Public/Ask browser identifier: a random visitor ID stored in your browser and sent to our service for rate limiting, consent/terms records and abuse prevention.
  • Signed-in Ask data: where you sign in, the student/account identifier associated with saved questions and conversation history.
  • Student Portal context: where needed to answer an account-related question, a limited account snapshot such as name or nickname, course, credit balance, upcoming classes and recent or pending payment status.
  • Terms/consent record: visitor or account identifier, version accepted, language and timestamp where we record acceptance.

Do not enter passwords, national ID or passport numbers, full payment-card or bank-account details, health data or other sensitive information unless a LITALK feature explicitly requires it for a lawful purpose. If you include such information in a chat message, it may be stored as part of that message until deleted.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We distinguish agreeing to the AI Chat Terms from consent under data-protection law. Acceptance of Terms creates or records the rules for using the feature; it is not automatically consent to every processing activity.

  • Contract or steps requested by you: providing the AI feature and responding to account-related questions for enrolled users where this is part of the requested service.
  • Legitimate interests: maintaining conversation context, preventing abuse, enforcing rate limits, securing the service, troubleshooting, reviewing quality and improving LITALK's assistant and teaching materials, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligations: retaining or disclosing information where required by applicable law.
  • Consent: only where consent is the appropriate legal basis for a specific processing purpose. Where used, consent may be withdrawn subject to applicable law and any independent lawful retention basis.

4. Who processes AI chat data

  • Google, through the Gemini API / Google Cloud services used to generate responses. Relevant message content and recent conversation context are sent for inference; Student Portal chats may also include the limited account snapshot described above.
  • Cloudflare, which provides application, hosting, security and database infrastructure used by the AI service.
  • Auth0 / Okta where authentication is used to associate a signed-in user with authorised Student Portal or Ask features.
  • Authorised LITALK staff who need access for support, safety, quality review or service improvement.

LITALK does not sell AI chat data or use it for third-party advertising. LITALK does not intentionally submit your conversations for training a third-party foundation model; provider-side processing remains subject to the configuration and contractual terms of the API service we use.

5. International transfers

AI and infrastructure providers may process data outside Thailand. Where the PDPA's cross-border rules apply, LITALK will rely on an available lawful transfer basis, appropriate safeguards or another mechanism permitted under Sections 28 and 29 of the PDPA and applicable subordinate rules.

6. Retention

LITALK's intended operational retention period for AI chat messages is six months from the message date, after which chat content is scheduled for deletion from the live chat store. Separate records, such as a terms-acceptance record, security record or account identifier, may have a different retention period where reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or required by law. If technical or legal circumstances require a temporary exception, we will limit continued retention to what is reasonably necessary.

7. Children and minors

LITALK serves learners of different ages. We do not assume that all processing of a minor's data must rely on parental consent. Where consent is the applicable legal basis and the data subject is a minor, we obtain and manage consent in accordance with Section 20 of the PDPA and applicable Thai law. Where another lawful basis applies, such as performance of a learning-service contract or a legal obligation, that basis may be used instead. A legally authorised parent or guardian may contact us regarding a minor's applicable data-protection rights.

8. Your rights and deletion

Rights available under the PDPA, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where applicable, are described in our Privacy Policy. Signed-in users may also have in-product controls to delete saved conversations. A deletion request may be subject to lawful exceptions or records we must retain independently.

9. Changes

We may update this notice when the AI service, providers, retention practices or legal requirements change. Material changes may require additional notice, a renewed Terms acceptance or consent where applicable.

10. Contact

For AI privacy questions or data-protection requests, contact support@litalkeducation.com or our official support channels.
