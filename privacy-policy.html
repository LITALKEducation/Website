Privacy Policy

This policy explains how LITALK Education handles personal data across our website, learning services, Student Portal and related features under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Last Updated: August 25, 2026 · Effective Date: August 25, 2026

1. Who we are

LITALK Education ("LITALK", "we", "us" or "our") is the data controller for personal data we determine how and why to process through our Services. This policy applies to our public website, learning programs, Student Portal, account services, attendance features and other LITALK-operated services. Separate notices may provide additional detail for a particular feature.

For AI chat, please also read our AI Chat Privacy Notice and AI Chat Terms.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, nickname, date of birth where required, email, phone number, student ID, and parent or guardian contact details where applicable.
  • Profile and media data: profile photo and other profile information you choose to provide.
  • Account and authentication data: account identifier, email, authentication-provider identifiers, account type and session or authentication metadata. Authentication for the Student Portal is provided using Auth0. LITALK does not store your Auth0 password in our application database.
  • Learning data: program enrolment, class schedules, attendance, study logs, lesson records, assessment or placement results, progress information, instructor feedback, course credit balance and related learning records.
  • Payment and transaction data: payment status, pending payments, transaction history, billing information and receipts. Payment providers may process additional payment credentials; LITALK does not intentionally store full card numbers in our application database.
  • Attendance and check-in data: records generated when a rotating QR code or check-in code is used for a class or event.
  • Communications: enquiries, messages and support communications sent through email, LINE Official Account, forms, the Student Portal or other support channels.
  • Technical and preference data: IP address where available to our systems or service providers, browser and device information, security logs, language and theme preferences, local-storage identifiers and related usage data.
  • AI chat data, where you use an AI feature, as described in the AI Chat Privacy Notice.

3. Why we process personal data and our legal bases

Depending on the activity, we process personal data on one or more lawful bases available under the PDPA. We do not treat acceptance of our Terms as consent for every processing activity.

  • Contract and steps before entering a contract: enrolment, account administration, scheduling, lessons, credits, payments and delivery of requested services.
  • Legal obligations: accounting, tax, regulatory or other records we must retain or disclose under applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests: operating and securing our services, preventing abuse and fraud, troubleshooting, service improvement, and protecting LITALK, learners and staff, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where the PDPA or another applicable law requires consent for a specific purpose. You may withdraw consent for that purpose, subject to applicable legal consequences and retention duties.

4. How we obtain data

We obtain data directly from you or your parent or guardian, from authorised staff who administer learning services, from your use of LITALK systems, and from service providers or partners involved in authentication, payments, communications, learning delivery or other requested functions.

5. Service providers and recipients

We disclose personal data only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to appropriate access restrictions and contractual or legal safeguards where applicable.

  • Authorised LITALK instructors and staff.
  • Auth0 / Okta for authentication, account session management and access control.
  • Cloudflare for hosting, security, edge services and database or application infrastructure used by LITALK systems.
  • Google and other learning or communications providers where used for features such as AI processing, video conferencing or productivity services. AI-specific disclosures are described in the AI Chat Privacy Notice.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers involved in processing a transaction.
  • LINE and other communications providers where you choose to contact us through those services.
  • Authorities, professional advisers or other recipients where disclosure is required or permitted by law, or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

6. Cookies and local storage

Our website and Student Portal use browser storage for essential functions and preferences, including language, theme, authentication/session support, AI visitor identifiers and records that a browser accepted the current AI terms. See our Cookie & Local Storage Policy for details.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may process personal data outside Thailand. Where the PDPA's cross-border transfer rules apply, LITALK will use an available lawful transfer basis, appropriate safeguards or another mechanism permitted under Sections 28 and 29 of the PDPA and applicable subordinate rules. The mechanism used may depend on the recipient, destination and circumstances of the transfer.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for any additional period required or permitted by applicable law. Retention depends on the data category, account or enrolment status, contractual obligations, security needs and accounting or tax requirements. We maintain internal retention rules and delete, de-identify or anonymise data when it is no longer required, unless a lawful reason requires continued retention.

AI chat messages have a specific retention period described in the AI Chat Privacy Notice.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the data and service, including authenticated access controls, encryption in transit where supported, restricted staff access and security measures provided by our infrastructure and identity providers. No service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your PDPA rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have rights to access and receive a copy of your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion or anonymisation, restrict processing, object to certain processing, request portability where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and lodge a complaint with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee or Office.

11. Children and minors

Many LITALK learners are minors. We do not rely on parental consent for every processing activity merely because a learner is a minor; for example, some processing may be necessary to provide a contracted learning service or comply with law. Where consent is the applicable legal basis and the data subject is a minor, LITALK will obtain and manage that consent in accordance with Section 20 of the PDPA and other applicable Thai law. A parent, guardian or person legally authorised to act for a minor may contact us regarding the minor's data and applicable rights.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated version with a new Last Updated date. Where a change materially affects your rights or how we use personal data, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by law.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise a data-protection right, contact LITALK Education at support@litalkeducation.com or through our official support channels.
