Cookie & Local Storage Policy

This policy explains the browser storage and similar technologies LITALK uses to operate, secure and remember preferences across our services.

Last Updated: August 25, 2026 · Effective Date: August 25, 2026

1. What this policy covers

LITALK uses cookies, local storage and similar browser technologies for essential service operation, authentication support, security, user preferences and AI-service controls. This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. Browser storage we currently use

  • litalk-lang — remembers the site's language preference.
  • theme — remembers a light or dark theme preference in the Student Portal.
  • litalk_visitor_id — stores a random browser identifier used by AI chat for rate limiting and abuse prevention. By itself it is not intended to identify you by name.
  • litalk_chat_terms_accepted — remembers the AI Chat Terms version accepted in that browser. LITALK may also keep a server-side record of the acceptance as described in the AI Chat Privacy Notice.
  • Authentication storage — Auth0 may use browser storage, including local storage and cookies where applicable, to maintain authenticated sessions and refresh-token functionality. These items are security-sensitive and should not be manually copied or shared.

3. Essential vs optional technologies

Storage used for authentication, security, rate limiting and core service operation may be necessary for a requested feature to work. Preference storage, such as language or theme, is used to remember choices. If LITALK later introduces non-essential analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking technologies that require consent under applicable law, we will provide an appropriate choice mechanism before using them where required.

4. How long browser storage remains

Some browser-storage items remain until deleted, replaced or cleared by you or the application; others expire or rotate according to the relevant service provider's session and security settings. Clearing browser storage may sign you out, reset preferences, remove an AI visitor identifier, or require you to accept the current AI terms again.

5. Your controls

Most browsers let you inspect, delete or block cookies and local storage. Blocking necessary authentication or security storage may prevent some LITALK features from working correctly. You can also change language and theme through the controls provided in our interface.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when storage technologies or our services change. Questions may be sent to support@litalkeducation.com.
